# Provenance and review

ThinFilmBook was drafted for this candidate as original English and Korean educational content. The bibliography links public technical sources for further study; it is not a source of copied prose, figure art, tables, datasets, or software. No publisher material is embedded in the generated site.

## Reference scope

The research set includes thin-film nucleation and growth, ALD surface chemistry, sputtered-film structure, CVD transport, epitaxial mismatch, stress, and NIST metrology publications. Each chapter declares its cited reference IDs, and inline citations link to the local reference record. The local reference-overlap review with My Library records ThinFilmBook as `original-deeper-treatment`; ProcessBook and DopingBook remain reference-only comparison points at their pinned revisions.

Bibliographic records and links were checked against publisher, institutional, or NIST records during authoring. The NIST web-page record includes its access date and the page's stated update date; its contents may change. This record does not claim a complete rights audit for the linked works or certify every technical interpretation.

## Technical review boundaries

The chapters distinguish physical mechanisms, reduced equations, and material/process-specific behavior. Numerical examples are illustrative. The three lab models include units, assumptions, bounded inputs, limiting-case checks, and stated omissions. No model is presented as calibrated equipment behavior or as a process window.

Independent internal reviewer feedback is editorial evidence only. A future release should retain the exact review findings, repair any in-scope P0/P1 issues, rerun all checks, and then obtain publication authorization. Local build and browser success do not demonstrate production deployment or fab validation.
